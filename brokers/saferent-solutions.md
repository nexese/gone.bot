# SafeRent Solutions

- ID: `saferent-solutions`
- Category: specialty reporting — tenant screening
- Jurisdictions: United States
- Review status: reviewed manual freeze guidance
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-17

## Official sources

- Consumer support and file-freeze forms: <https://saferentsolutions.com/consumer-support/>
- CFPB 2025 company list: <https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_consumer-reporting-companies_list_2025.pdf>

## Supported goals

- `freeze` — SafeRent will freeze its consumer report on request when deletion is unavailable. This restricts release and does not erase the file.

## Channels and required capabilities

- Channel: current official consumer-relations channel, telephone, or postal mail
- Harness capabilities: browser and filesystem; phone/postal actions are manual
- Destination: start at SafeRent's consumer-support page

## Minimum known fields

Use only current official requirements. The user handles identifiers, documents, signature, and recovery information directly.

## Procedure

1. Explain that the freeze may delay or prevent a legitimate landlord from completing a rental screen.
2. Confirm no imminent rental application requires access.
3. Verify the channel, preview the request, and obtain approval.
4. The user completes sensitive and final steps; record minimal evidence.

## Manual checkpoints

- Phone or mail, identifiers, documents, signature, verification, and final submission are user-handled.

## Evidence and status interpretation

A sent request is not an active freeze until confirmed. A freeze is not deletion and does not remove source records.

## Limitations

- The SafeRent freeze covers data SafeRent maintains, such as housing-court and additional-address information; it does not freeze resold Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion credit data.
- Review current access exceptions, product scope, and lift/removal instructions.
