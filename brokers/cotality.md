# Cotality

- ID: `cotality`
- Category: property data and specialty reporting
- Jurisdictions: privacy rights vary by residence; U.S. Credco consumer assistance is separate
- Review status: reviewed entry-point guidance
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-17

## Official sources

- California privacy notice and request links: <https://www.cotality.com/legal/california-notice-at-collection>
- Credco consumer assistance: <https://www.cotality.com/legal/credco-consumer-assistance>

## Supported goals

- `delete` and `opt-out` — use the choices offered by Cotality's live privacy portal for the user's residence, including sale or sharing opt-out where available.
- `freeze` — not represented as a Credco control. Cotality says Credco resells merged reports rather than maintaining the source database; restrict source credit files through the applicable source-bureau profiles instead.

## Channels and required capabilities

- Channel: browser privacy portal or official telephone option
- Harness capabilities: browser and filesystem; email or phone may be needed for verification
- Destination: start from the current Cotality privacy notice

## Minimum known fields

The routed official portal determines required identity and contact fields. The user enters sensitive values directly.

## Procedure

1. Use Cotality's privacy portal for applicable deletion or opt-out choices.
2. If the concern is a Credco merged credit report and no removal choice applies, explain that separate source-bureau freezes are the available restriction path.
3. Never claim that one source-bureau freeze covers the others; obtain approval separately.
4. Record the exact entity, control, scope, and reported result.

## Manual checkpoints

- Identity verification, attestations, sensitive fields, CAPTCHA, and final submission are user-handled.

## Evidence and status interpretation

A privacy response may be partial or exempt. A source-bureau freeze restricts that bureau's report; it does not delete Cotality data or prove that Credco can no longer produce every product.

## Limitations

- Consumer-reporting, GLBA, public-record, service-provider, and other data may be exempt from a privacy deletion request.
- Follow the current official notice because available rights and brands can change.
