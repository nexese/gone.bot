# Blend Insights

- ID: `blend-insights`
- Category: specialty reporting — employment, income, and bank-asset verification
- Jurisdictions: United States
- Review status: reviewed manual freeze guidance
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-17

## Official sources

- Blend: <https://blend.com/>
- CFPB 2025 company list: <https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_consumer-reporting-companies_list_2025.pdf>

## Supported goals

- `freeze` — the CFPB listing says Blend Insights will freeze its consumer report on request. This restricts release rather than erasing information.

## Channels and required capabilities

- Channel: current official consumer channel or the CFPB-listed telephone/postal route
- Harness capabilities: browser and filesystem; telephone/postal action is manual
- Destination: verify Blend's current consumer instructions before disclosing information

## Minimum known fields

Use only the current official requirements. The user directly supplies identifiers, documents, signatures, and authentication information.

## Procedure

1. Explain that Blend reports can support lender verification and a freeze may delay a legitimate credit application.
2. Confirm the user's intended consequence and check for imminent applications.
3. Verify the current channel; preview fields, destination, and request.
4. Obtain approval and let the user complete sensitive/final steps.

## Manual checkpoints

- Telephone/postal handling, identifiers, documents, signature, and final submission are user-handled.

## Evidence and status interpretation

A request is not an active freeze until confirmed. The freeze is not deletion and does not freeze source bank, employer, or credit-bureau records.

## Limitations

- The CFPB says a Blend report may exist only after a lender using Blend has recently considered the user.
- Recheck Blend's official consumer channel before use.
