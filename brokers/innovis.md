# Innovis

- ID: `innovis`
- Category: supplementary consumer reporting
- Jurisdictions: United States
- Review status: reviewed freeze guidance
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-17

## Official sources

- Security freeze: <https://www.innovis.com/personal/securityFreeze>
- CFPB company list: <https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/>

## Supported goals

- `freeze` — restrict access to the Innovis consumer report when deletion is unavailable. This does not erase the report.

## Channels and required capabilities

- Channel: official browser page, telephone, or postal mail
- Harness capabilities: browser and filesystem; user handles identity verification
- Destination: Innovis security-freeze page

## Minimum known fields

Use the current official channel. Sensitive identifiers, documents, PINs, and recovery information remain user-handled.

## Procedure

1. Explain that Innovis is separate from the three nationwide bureaus.
2. Warn about possible delays to legitimate credit or identity-verification activity.
3. Preview, approve, and let the user complete the official request.
4. Record minimal confirmation evidence and lift instructions.

## Manual checkpoints

- Sensitive fields, identity documents, CAPTCHA, authentication, and final submission are user-handled.

## Evidence and status interpretation

An Innovis confirmation is not deletion and does not establish a freeze elsewhere.

## Limitations

- Review the live notice for permitted access and current lift/removal procedures.
