# National Consumer Telecom & Utilities Exchange (NCTUE)

- ID: `nctue`
- Category: specialty reporting — telecom and utilities
- Jurisdictions: United States
- Review status: reviewed freeze guidance
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-17

## Official sources

- Consumer information and freeze: <https://nctue.com/consumer/>
- CFPB company list: <https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/>

## Supported goals

- `freeze` — restrict release of the NCTUE report when deletion is unavailable. This does not erase the file.
- `opt-out` — use the distinct prescreen choice only when it matches the user's goal; it is not a report freeze.

## Channels and required capabilities

- Channel: official browser guidance, telephone, or postal mail
- Harness capabilities: browser and filesystem; postal/phone steps are manual
- Destination: NCTUE consumer page

## Minimum known fields

Use the current official form or instructions. The user handles identifiers, documents, signatures, and recovery information directly.

## Procedure

1. Keep the NCTUE freeze separate from its prescreen opt-out.
2. Warn that a freeze may affect legitimate telecom, cable, or utility applications and deposits.
3. Preview the specific channel and obtain approval.
4. The user completes sensitive and final steps; record only minimal evidence.

## Manual checkpoints

- Telephone/postal handling, sensitive fields, identity documents, signature, and final submission are user-handled.

## Evidence and status interpretation

A confirmation proves only NCTUE's reported restriction. It is not deletion and does not freeze Equifax or another report.

## Limitations

- NCTUE is serviced by Equifax, but the NCTUE freeze is a separate control.
- Review current access exceptions and lift instructions.
