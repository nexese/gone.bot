# Security-freeze request

Prefer the company's official freeze portal or form. Use this text only for a reviewed email or postal channel.

Subject: Request to place a security freeze on my consumer report

Hello,

I am asking you to place a security freeze on the consumer report your organization maintains about me. I am acting for myself.

Please use only the information required by your official process to locate and verify my file. I will provide sensitive identifiers or identity documents directly through the reviewed secure channel rather than in ordinary email.

Please confirm when the freeze is active and provide the official instructions for temporarily lifting or removing it.

Thank you,

[name, only if required]

Do not put a Social Security number, government-identifier value, identity-document copy, PIN, recovery code, or knowledge-based-authentication answer in chat or the progress file. The user completes those fields directly in the official channel.
